## How to read it

- chain / MOP near 1 on south swell and well above 1 on W–WNW says the excess §56 found is in the chain's wrap round Point Loma, not shared by the established model.
- Near 1 everywhere says the two ray models agree, and the excess, if the beach shows one, is in what both leave out (dissipation over the shelf, §56's other half) or in the ray method itself.
- Neither says what the beach did. Only the observation log can.
