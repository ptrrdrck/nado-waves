## 1. Ridge to ridge: cannot resolve it

A ridge's frequency climbs g/(4πR): 5.6e-04 Hz/h from 5,000 km, 1.9e-04 from 15,000. A 5 h lag therefore shifts a ridge by 0.0009–0.0028 Hz, under a third of one 0.005 Hz bin, and the refined peaks scatter ~0.001–0.002 Hz about their line.

Measured: 218 pairs of ridges (≥ 10 points each, overlapping ≥ 0.004 Hz, within 36 h). Lag median +3.5 h, interquartile -10.0 to +16.1 h, against a prediction of 2–6 h. **Dropped**: the time a ridge reaches a frequency is too soft to time a few hours by.

## What the briefing may say

- **A time, from physics, labelled as such**: when 46047's west or north-west sector has risen, the deep-water travel time to 46232 for its period and heading, then the rays' leg to each break. The archive agrees with it on average (§3); nothing here can time one swell (§1–2), so it is "due", never "arrives at", and never confirmed at 46232 by this method.
- **Never a height**: nothing here measures how much of 46047's swell survives the Channel Islands to 46232; the breaks' heights come from 46232's own spectrum once it is there.
- **Not past 17 s, not south**: unmeasured and wrong-signed respectively.
