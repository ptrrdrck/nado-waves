## What this does not say

- **Nothing is observed.** A model's friction with an unread roughness laid over the whole of a path; where the rock is, and what roughness it has, are both unknown here.
- **An upper bound in extent, not in roughness.** The rough bed covers every metre of each wrap path, deep water and sand included; a real reef covers part. A rougher k_N than 0.5 m would take more.
- **Not a fix.** Fitting k_N to make the headline agree with anything would be calibration, which is the beach log's.
