The Coronado aperture

Every vertex the forecast's geometry uses, drawn where it sits and joined the way the model joins it. Each coloured ray runs from a break to the one vertex that forms that edge of its swell window — the vertex whose error would move the edge. This is the model, not the chart: the stored ENC extracts hold thousands of coastline vertices, and the aperture is decided by a few dozen.

Windows, and the vertex behind each edge

Bearings are the direction swell comes FROM. “100 m moves it” is how far the edge swings if that one vertex is wrong by 100 m (328 ft) — the leverage of each coordinate.
