Rideable

About the face, not the size — two feet and clean is not two feet closing out. Yes: waves had a rideable face, you could catch and ride them. Marginal: a few rideable, most closing out, or barely enough push to get in. No: nothing rideable — closing out, too small to catch, or too blown out.
